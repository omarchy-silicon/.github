# Code of Conduct

Omarchy Silicon is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and professional.
- Focus criticism on ideas, implementations, and evidence rather than people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Keep hardware, security, and support discussions factual and reproducible.
- Respect the applicable licenses and upstream communities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publishing private or security-sensitive information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainers through the central platform repository: https://github.com/omarchy-silicon/omarchy-apple-platform/security/advisories/new

Do not use a public issue for a conduct report. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as the circumstances permit.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and activities operated by Omarchy Silicon. Repository-specific policies and upstream codes of conduct may also apply.
